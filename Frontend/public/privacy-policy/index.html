Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Testify is a technology platform that facilitates booking of basic wellness checks conducted by independent service providers in Delhi.

Information We Collect

Basic account details (name, email/phone), booking details (service and location), and payment status. We do not store full payment card details.

Use of Information

To provide and improve services, process bookings, facilitate payments, and communicate updates related to your bookings.

Payments

Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We receive payment status and references from Razorpay to reconcile bookings. We do not store complete card or UPI details.

Sharing

Necessary booking details may be shared with independent providers to fulfill your request. We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Location & Maps

We use Google Maps to assist with address and approximate location for provider routing. You can control location permissions via your device settings.

Cookies & Analytics

Basic cookies and analytics may be used to improve site performance and reliability. You can manage cookies via your browser settings.

Your Rights

Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data via our Contact page. For privacy queries, include your booking ID and payment reference where applicable.
