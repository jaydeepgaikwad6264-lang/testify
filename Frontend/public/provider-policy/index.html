Provider Terms & Policies

Professional guidelines for Testify service providers operating in Delhi NCR.

Conduct & Compliance

  • Maintain punctuality and professional behavior during all visits.
  • Carry required equipment and ensure accuracy of readings.
  • Respect user privacy and do not share user data.
  • Only operate within Delhi NCR serviceable areas.

Payments & Wallet

  • Wallet credits: ₹35 per BP/Sugar order, ₹50 per Combo order.
  • Credits reflect upon completion status update.
  • Payouts are processed per company schedule.

Cancellations

  • Cancelled orders do not earn credits.
  • Repeated cancellations may affect account standing.
