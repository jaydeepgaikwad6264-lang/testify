About Testify

Testify is a technology platform that facilitates booking of basic wellness checks conducted by independent service providers in Delhi.

Our Mission

Make basic wellness checks accessible, affordable, and convenient for everyone in Delhi. We connect users with nearby providers who can visit at a suitable time.

How It Works

  • Select a service (BP Check, Sugar Check, Combo).
  • Provide your location and preferred time.
  • A provider arrives within 30–45 minutes.
Testify does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
